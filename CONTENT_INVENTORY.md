## Lesson and syllabus scope

Every CISCE/ISC lesson contains overview, learning objectives, explanatory sections, worked example, exam focus, misconceptions and retrieval checks. Extension unit pages contain topic checklists, assessment targets, links to authored core lessons and track-specific practice. They are mapped study packs, not 65 additional standalone long-form manuscripts.

## Track date policy

- CISCE: verify each new council publication.
- CBSE: academic-year official curriculum is the source of truth.
- JEE Main: verify NTA information bulletin/syllabus for the relevant cycle.
- NEET: the 2026 official syllabus is the current recorded baseline; verify 2027 before using for 2027 exam preparation.
- JEE Advanced: enrichment layer, not a separately verified official syllabus in this release.

## V5 implementation-status policy

The 50-feature registry records only what the source currently contains. Features marked planned remain locked. The V5 neutral-equation balancer is one of the 15 available features and supports a restricted neutral molecular formula grammar; ionic/redox and hydrate balancing are not supported. The presence of a roadmap item does not count as implementation. See `docs/V5_ADVANCED_FEATURES.md` and `docs/V5_BUG_AUDIT.md`.
