# Chemistry Atlas V7 — frontier engineering vision

V7 is a long-range research target, not a feature-completion promise. Every workstream needs independent scientific, performance, safety, accessibility and licensing gates.

## 1. Multi-fidelity Chemical Digital Twin

A unified scene switches between pedagogical geometry, empirical force-field models, molecular mechanics, semi-empirical methods and selected quantum-chemistry reference calculations. The UI shows method, computational cost, uncertainty and domain of validity. Never present a force-field trajectory as a quantum calculation.

## 2. Reaction Pathway and Kinetics Lab

Atom-mapped reactions, mechanism graphs, reaction networks, energy profiles and microkinetic models. Mechanisms are labelled as experimentally established, computationally proposed or simplified teaching pathways. Conservation of mass, atoms and charge are tested automatically.

## 3. Live Laboratory Digital Twin

A safe integration layer for approved school sensors and instruments: pH, temperature, conductivity, absorbance and mass. Calibrations, uncertainty, units and sensor metadata are stored beside readings. A virtual twin visualises a teacher-approved experiment; it does not direct unsupervised hazardous procedures.

## 4. Chemistry Knowledge Graph

A typed, versioned graph links elements, compounds, structures, reactions, spectra, properties, experiments, misconceptions, curriculum outcomes and primary references. Students can trace an observation to a model, equation and evidence source.

## 5. AI Scientific Co-Pilot

A tool-using tutor calls deterministic chemistry calculators, parsers and approved references. It returns citations, units, assumptions and uncertainty; it must not fabricate unsupported mechanisms or values. Human teacher approval gates publication of AI-generated material.

## 6. Browser scientific computing

Use WebGPU/WebAssembly where supported, with tested CPU fallback and low-memory modes. Stream progressive levels of detail; never require a discrete GPU for core study paths. Optional worker computation is budget-controlled, opt-in and transparent.

## 7. Evidence Ledger and Model Cards

Every published model receives provenance, data checksum, versioned equations, validation suite, limitations, reviewers and change history. Failed validation blocks publication.

## 8. Reproducible Student Research Workbench

Students formulate a question, define variables, capture evidence, perform uncertainty-aware analysis, cite data and export reproducible reports. Teacher review and privacy-safe sharing are built in.

## 9. Cross-device Immersive Classroom

A synchronized teacher-led 3D scene for phones, laptops and projectors; AR/VR is optional. All essential learning tasks remain available without headsets or high-end graphics hardware.

## 10. Chemistry Atlas Bench

A public, versioned benchmark suite for molecular geometry, unit handling, reaction balancing, calculator accuracy, provenance, accessibility, mobile rendering and learning outcomes. Publish tests and failures, not unsupported “best in the world” claims.

## What would make V7 exceptional

The differentiator is not one photorealistic model. It is a traceable loop from real data → validated model → interactive experiment → learner reasoning → assessment → reproducible evidence, with each transformation versioned and independently testable.
