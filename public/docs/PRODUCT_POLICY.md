# Chemistry Atlas permanent product policy

## Permanent learner-access promise

Chemistry Atlas is intended to remain **free, non-commercial and ad-free permanently**. This is a product requirement, not a temporary launch promotion.

- No subscription, paid tier, paywall, paid model pack or paid classroom feature.
- No display advertising, sponsored ranking, behavioural advertising, affiliate placement or sale of learner data.
- No mandatory paid AI provider or commercial API for core study paths. When a free service is unavailable or quota-limited, the affected optional feature should show a clear offline/disabled state; core lessons, revision and local learning tools must remain usable.
- No degradation of core educational content to force a payment.
- Donations or grants, if ever used, must be voluntary, transparent and must not influence scientific content, learner ranking or access.

## Important operating-cost distinction

“Free to learners” does not guarantee that hosting, domains, data transfer, storage, moderation or optional AI computation cost the operator nothing. Prefer open standards, static hosting, caching, local computation and no-cost quotas, and design graceful fallbacks. Never silently introduce a charge or a paid dependency. Infrastructure cost/limit notices belong in the maintainer deployment guide, not as a paywall in the student experience.

## Non-commercial use and source-code licence

The hosted Chemistry Atlas product is non-commercial by owner decision. The GitHub repository currently has an MIT licence selected at repository creation. MIT permits commercial reuse of the source code; that legal licence is separate from the intended operation of the hosted product. Do not represent the MIT licence as a non-commercial source licence, and do not change it without a separate explicit owner/legal decision.

Scientific datasets, spectra, imagery and software dependencies each retain their own licence. A source being free to access does not automatically permit bulk redistribution. Store source, record identifier, version, retrieval date, licence snapshot, attribution, units and SHA-256 for any imported dataset.

## Learner privacy and safety

- Collect only what is necessary for accounts, learning progress, teacher feedback and security.
- Never sell student data or use it for targeted advertising.
- Teacher analytics should remain aggregate by default; exclude private notes, portfolio bodies and AI conversation text.
- Keep student portfolios private to the school and their permitted reviewers.
- Provide export/deletion and retention controls before real-school onboarding.
- Publish only age-appropriate, teacher-reviewed practical guidance. Hazardous procedures must not be adapted into unsupervised home activities.

## Scientific claims

Every model must describe whether it is experimental, computational or idealised, including method, data provenance, units, assumptions, uncertainty, validation status and known limitations. Never claim all results are exact or that the platform is “the world's best” without independent benchmarks and learning-outcome evidence.

This is a product-policy statement and engineering requirement, not legal advice or a completed privacy notice/terms of service.
